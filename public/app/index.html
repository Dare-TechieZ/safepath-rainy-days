🛡️ Travel Safety Card

Before travelling

  • Check rainfall intensity before you leave.
  • Avoid flooded underpasses entirely.
  • Do not enter roads where the water depth is unknown.
  • Keep your phone charged and carry a power bank.
  • Carry emergency supplies (water, torch, basic first aid).
  • Inform someone about your route and expected arrival.

During heavy rainfall

  • Reduce speed and switch on low-beam headlights.
  • Maintain extra braking distance.
  • Avoid driving through moving water.
  • Do not stop under trees, hoardings or unsafe structures.
  • Follow instructions from local authorities and traffic police.
⚠️ If water is moving rapidly across the road, DO NOT attempt to cross.
